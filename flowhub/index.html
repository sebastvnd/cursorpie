How it works

  1. PickOpen a card to see what the flow does and what it needs.
  2. InstallWith the extension and CursorPie running, one tap. CursorPie asks you first, then adds it as a new flow.
  3. Or downloadNo extension, or CursorPie is closed? You get the .json file: Settings > Flow > Import.
Submissions are closed for now. Only the author adds flows while Flow Hub is in beta. Every flow is a plain, readable file, and a flow never runs by itself: it only runs when you give it to a slice.
